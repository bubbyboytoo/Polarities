IMPORTANT! This version of Polarities is an unstable release. You can think of it as an open beta. Please report all bugs in our discord!

Polarities has finally been ported in full (mostly) to 1.4.4! However, the work is far from over: We're releasing this mostly to fulfill a promise to the people we've kept waiting, and the mod will absolutely be receiving bugfixes galore and new content in future updates, as this represents a release with all the content, but none of the quality assurance.

Polarities is a content mod which aims to add mechanically interesting bosses, enemies, items, and more! Currently adds ten new bosses, several new enemies, a variety of weapons and accessories, some tiles and armor sets, two new mini-biomes, a new subworld, and a new town NPC.
